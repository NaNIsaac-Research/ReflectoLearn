# G2 run notes

The verdicts are in [g2_report.md](g2_report.md), generated by `experiments/005_fisher_global/g2_run.py report`.
This file gives the run's cost and two descriptive checks, computed from the 475 result files; no number
here decides a verdict ([PREREG-G2.md](../../paper/prereg/PREREG-G2.md)).

## Cost against the estimate

The pre-registration estimated about 9 CPU hours and 2.3 hours of wall time on 4 processes, at 34 s per
refinement. The run used 62.3 CPU hours: 58.9 h in the 950 engine refinements and 3.4 h in the
polishes on the converged forward (median 24 s per draw, close to the 15 s per polish assumed).

The median refinement took 14 s, below the 34 s assumed. The miss is the tail: 137 of the 950
refinements (14 %) ran to the budget of 3000 engine evaluations, and they account for 81 % of the
engine time (mean 223 s per refinement, 90th percentile 214 s, longest 2.5 h). Outcomes: 839
confirmed, 106 stopped at the budget, 5 stalled. No run segment came near the 16 h wall limit, and
peak memory was 0.89 GB per process.

Wall time: the job started on 2026-11-13 at 03:34 and the last draw was written on 2026-11-15 at 07:22.
The workers ran for about 20.5 h of that, summed over 48 run segments (`run_g2/resources_segment_*.json`).
The rest of the time the job was paused or queued so that other test-queue jobs could run first:
the queue guard paused it 60 times. From 14:25 to 15:58 on 2026-11-13 the guard waited for an empty
queue before restarting the job, while other preemptible jobs held the queue one after another, so the job
did not start again. After commit 09f431f the guard queues the job again straight after a pause. Draws
carry the commit they were computed with (8377369 before the change, 09f431f after); the two commits
differ only in the guard script.

## Budget-limited fits and the verdicts

H-N2 and H-N3 count draws whose truth fit or basin fit stopped at the budget. A truth fit stopped
early leaves D_truth too high and G too low, which works against the hypothesis. A basin fit stopped
early leaves D_basin too high and G too high, which works in its favour.

| set (S₁\* ≤ 10⁴) | draws | basin fit not confirmed | smallest G among those | draws with both fits confirmed | G ≥ 25 among those |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Poisson | 285 | 15 | 2134 | 214 | 214 |
| negative-binomial | 87 | 5 | 35525 | 64 | 64 |

The smallest G over all these draws is 1736 (Poisson) and 1720 (negative-binomial), so no budget
stop is near the threshold of 25.

## H-N1 by basin distance

H-N1 fails (0.366 of 385 Poisson draws). Split by the candidate's noise-free S₁\*:

| candidates | draws | noisy minimum within cross-deviance 25 of the recorded one | median cross-deviance | median ΔD₀ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S₁\* > 10⁴ (the 5 near basins) | 100 | 0.99 | 8.9 | 11.7 |
| S₁\* ≤ 10⁴ | 285 | 0.15 | 1292 | 42757 |

Restricted to the 365 draws whose basin fit was confirmed, the fraction is 0.370. Inference: the far
basins are minima of the search box (most rest on a bound) and move under noise by more than 25 deviance
units while staying far from the truth; the threshold of 25 is absolute, not relative to ΔD₀. The near
basins stay where they were.
