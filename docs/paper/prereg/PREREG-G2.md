# Pre-registration G2, G2b, G2c: noisy data, mismatched truth, bootstrap

Written 2026-11-06, before any run below. Nothing in this file has been run. The runs answer the
referee-style objections to the draft in [../paper_draft.md](../paper_draft.md) sections 0 and 6.5:
all current results are noise-free, generated by the fitted model (an inverse crime), and a deviance
margin is not a test.

Subsets are fixed by `experiments/005_fisher_global/prereg_subsets.py` (seed 20261107) from the
recorded basin run and stored in [g2_subset.json](g2_subset.json). Code for the runs is committed
before the first run and the commit hash is written into the result file.

## Common set-up

- Candidates are distinct R2a wrong basins, identified by their representative endpoint id
  (`basin_verdict.json`, field `rep`). X2e is not part of G2.
- The engine is `scan.expected_counts` (the engine of the recorded results), at S = 10⁶ counts at
  R = 1, with the recorded condition and truth. The objective is the Poisson deviance. The fit is the
  basin run's refinement (`basin.refine`: same budget N_max = 3000 evaluations and the convergence
  tests C1 to C4), started from the recorded basin minimum or, for the truth fit, from the truth.
- Seeds: every random draw uses `numpy.random.SeedSequence([20261107, <stage>, <basin index>, <draw>])`.
- Runs: 4 processes, one torch thread each, BelowNormal priority, a wrapper that stops at 4 GB per
  process or 16 h of wall time. Results are one file per candidate and draw, written once.

## Forward models (added before any run, after the G4 follow-up)

The experiment engine's resolution integral is a fixed 15-node Gauss-Hermite rule that does not
converge ([g4_diagnose.md](../../fisher_global/g4_diagnose.md)); the published ReflectoSim forward does, at about 70
times the cost per curve. So:

1. The search and the refinements named above run on the experiment engine, as registered.
2. Every refined minimum is then **polished on the converged forward**: a trust-region least-squares
   from the engine minimum with the residuals of the ReflectoSim adaptive-resolution forward, the engine's
   Jacobian as the approximate Jacobian, the same bounds, at most 30 forward evaluations, and its Poisson
   deviance recorded. Deviances of the polished minima are the **converged scores**.
3. Each hypothesis is evaluated twice, on the engine scores and on the converged scores. The converged
   scores are the headline. If the two disagree on a verdict, both are reported and the hypothesis is
   called unresolved.
4. Distinct basins are grouped by cross-deviance on the converged forward.

The polish adds about 4 CPU hours to G2 (950 polishes at about 15 s, inference); G2b and G2c add
about 1 and 3 hours.

## G2: do wrong basins stay rejected under noise?

Data: for each of the 100 candidates (the 5 basins with S₁\* ≥ 10³ and 95 drawn in proportion over
single-film and bilayer conditions), 3 Poisson draws of the condition's truth curve. 30 of the 100 also
get 3 negative-binomial draws with mean equal to the truth curve and variance (1 + (φ − 1)·1) × mean,
φ = 1.34 (the median of F9, draft). The 5 basins with S₁\* ≥ 10³ get 20 Poisson draws each instead of 3.

Per draw two refinements: from the truth and from the candidate basin's recorded minimum. Let
D_truth and D_basin be the deviances of the data at the two minima; the noisy gap is G = D_basin −
D_truth (both minima, same data). Let ΔD₀ be the noise-free deviance of the recorded basin.

| hypothesis | statement | holds | fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| H-N1 | the noisy minimum reached from the basin start is the same basin as the noise-free one (cross-deviance of the two minima at most 25) | at least 80 % of draws | below 60 % |
| H-N2 | basins with noise-free S₁\* ≤ 10⁴ stay rejected: G ≥ 25 | at least 95 % of those draws | below 80 % |
| H-N3 | the same as H-N2 on the overdispersed draws | at least 95 % | below 80 % (not testable with fewer than 20 candidates) |

Between the two bounds a hypothesis is unresolved. The descriptive output is the table of G
against ΔD₀ and the fraction of draws with G < 4 (not rejected) for the 5 near basins. No other
number decides a verdict.

Cost estimate (inference): 95 × 3 + 5 × 20 + 30 × 3 = 475 draws, two refinements each, at 34 s per
refinement (a refinement from a nearby minimum is closer to the recorded median of 26.5 s than to
the 241 s mean of the far starts), about 9 CPU hours; with 4 processes about 2.3 hours of wall time.

### Choices fixed before the G2 run (2026-11-12, written before any G2 fit)

The text above leaves these open; they are fixed here, in `experiments/005_fisher_global/g2_run.py`.

- Data curve. The draws are taken from the truth curve on the converged forward, as in G2b, so that the
  converged scores (the headline) are not offset by the engine's quadrature error. The engine is used
  for the refinements only, as registered.
- Seeds. Stage 1 for the Poisson draws and stage 3 for the negative-binomial draws (stage 2 is G2b); the
  basin index is the candidate's position in `g2` of the subset file, also for the `g2_phi` candidates.
- Negative-binomial draws: mean λ and variance φλ with φ = 1.34, numpy's parameters n = λ / (φ − 1) and
  p = 1 / φ per point.
- H-N1 compares the noisy basin minimum with the recorded noise-free basin minimum by cross-deviance on
  the converged forward, at most 25. ΔD₀ is the converged-forward deviance between the recorded basin
  minimum and the truth curve.
- Files: one result file per draw, written once; each of the two fits of a draw is also written to its
  own file when it ends, so a preempted run loses at most the fit in progress. Runs through the test
  queue as a preemptible job, 4 processes.
- Cost. A one-draw check before the run did not finish within 10 minutes, against the 70 s per draw
  assumed above, so the cost estimate is likely low by a large factor. The first hour of the run measures
  it; the run continues unless the queue owner stops it, and the report gives the number of draws done.

## G2b: mismatched truth

Removes the inverse crime. The data curve is not from the fitting model: one interface of the truth
(the film to substrate interface, or the film to oxide interface for stacks with an oxide) is replaced
by a graded transition of five equal slabs spanning 10 Å, with the SLD stepping linearly between the two
media; the curve is computed with ReflectoSim's forward and scaled by S. The fitting model is
unchanged (error-function roughness). 30 candidates (`g2b` in the subset file), one noise-free
dataset each, and one Poisson draw each.

Per candidate: a truth fit (from the truth parameters) and a basin fit (from the recorded minimum).
D_truth > 0 is the model-mismatch floor of that condition. The margin is M = D_basin − D_truth.

| hypothesis | statement | holds | fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| H-M1 | M ≥ 25 for candidates with noise-free S₁\* ≤ 10⁴ | at least 90 % of candidates | below 70 % |
| H-M2 | the mismatch floor does not exceed the margin: D_truth < M for the same candidates | at least 90 % | below 70 % |

Descriptive: the distribution of D_truth, which is the reduced χ² analogue of the noise-free
mismatch. Cost: 30 candidates × 2 datasets × 2 refinements at 34 s, about 1 CPU hour (inference).

## G2c: parametric bootstrap on the closest basins

The 10 basins with the largest S₁\* (`g2c_closest`), among them the five of the oxide merged into the
film. For each: 30 Poisson draws of the truth curve at 10⁶ counts; each is fitted from the truth and
from the basin minimum, and T = D_basin − D_truth is recorded (T > 0 means the truth is preferred).
With the basin as the generating structure, the same is done with 15 draws of the basin's curve
(starts: basin minimum and truth).

A second set of truth-generated draws (30 per basin) is overdispersed with the per-q-region φ measured
on the real repeats in [PREREG-G1.md](PREREG-G1.md), once that run exists; until then the 1.34 of F9
is used and the run is marked as such.

Outputs: the fraction of truth-generated draws with T > 0 (the power to prefer the truth), the fraction
of basin-generated draws with T < 0, and the quantiles of T against χ² of 12 free parameters
(95 % bound 21.0). No verdict rule: this is the reference for the statement that a deviance margin is
not a test, and for how many of the 10 basins are statistically distinguishable at 10⁶ counts.
Cost: (30 + 15) draws × 2 refinements × 10 basins at 34 s, about 8.5 CPU hours (inference).

## What would change the paper

- H-N2 fails or G2b's H-M1 fails: the central claim is restricted to noise-free, mismatch-free data and
  the abstract says so first.
- Both hold: the claim stays "for the tested classes" with noisy and mismatched data named as checked
  at these counts; measured data (G1) remain open.
- G2c shows the exception basins are not distinguishable at 10⁶ counts: the exception is stated as a
  limit of the experiment, with the count level at which it is resolved.
